# AED-00014

# Governance Health Metrics

---

# Status

**Proposed**

Normative.

---

# 1. Purpose

This AED defines how the repository measures and communicates its governance health.

AED-00011 defines compliance measurement at a point in time.

This AED defines how compliance is tracked over time.

A single Compliance Score is a snapshot.

A Governance Health Metric is a trend.

Trends reveal what snapshots cannot: whether the repository is improving, stable, or decaying.

---

# 2. Motivation

Governance can degrade gradually.

A repository may maintain an ACCEPTABLE Compliance Score while accumulating structural debt that will eventually produce a CRITICAL score.

Point-in-time measurement does not detect gradual degradation.

Trend measurement does.

Additionally, governance improvement requires evidence.

Without historical metrics, architectural decisions are made on intuition rather than data.

This AED establishes the data foundation for evidence-based governance evolution.

---

# 3. Core Principle

Governance health SHALL be measured continuously.

Measurement SHALL produce trends, not only snapshots.

Trends SHALL inform governance evolution.

Governance evolution SHALL be reflected in subsequent metrics.

This creates the feedback loop between enforcement, observation, and improvement.

---

# 4. Metrics Taxonomy

Repository governance health is measured through three metric tiers.

---

## Tier 1 — Primary Metrics

Primary metrics directly measure compliance.

They are computed on every enforcement run.

They are the canonical health indicators.

---

## Tier 2 — Secondary Metrics

Secondary metrics measure governance quality dimensions.

They are computed on every enforcement run.

They inform root cause analysis of primary metric changes.

---

## Tier 3 — Tertiary Metrics

Tertiary metrics measure governance maturity and coverage.

They are computed on a scheduled basis (daily or per architectural review).

They inform long-term governance strategy.

---

# 5. Primary Metrics

---

## PM-001: Compliance Score

Definition: the weighted enforcement score defined in AED-00011, Section 8.

Range: 0.0 – 1.0.

Target: ≥ 0.95 (HEALTHY).

Computed: every enforcement run.

Exposed: Repository Registry, Boot sequence, every Enforcement Report.

This is the canonical governance health indicator.

All other metrics exist to explain it.

---

## PM-002: Hard Violation Count

Definition: the number of unresolved Hard violations in the Violation Registry.

Range: 0 – N.

Target: 0.

Computed: every enforcement run.

A non-zero Hard Violation Count at a gate MUST block execution.

---

## PM-003: Soft Violation Count

Definition: the number of unresolved Soft violations in the Violation Registry.

Range: 0 – N.

Target: ≤ 5 at any given time.

Computed: every enforcement run.

Soft violations above target trigger a governance review recommendation.

---

## PM-004: Exception Debt Score

Definition: the total Compliance Score impact attributed to Active exceptions.

Formula: sum of exception penalties as defined in AED-00013, Section 11.

Range: 0.0 – 1.0.

Target: ≤ 0.10.

Computed: every enforcement run.

An Exception Debt Score above 0.15 triggers a Hard governance review.

---

# 6. Secondary Metrics

---

## SM-001: Drift Velocity

Definition: the rate of change in the Compliance Score between successive enforcement runs.

Formula:

```text
Drift Velocity = Compliance Score (current) − Compliance Score (previous)
```

Positive velocity indicates improvement.

Negative velocity indicates degradation.

Target: ≥ 0.00 (stable or improving).

Consecutive negative velocity readings trigger a Soft violation.

Three consecutive negative velocity readings trigger a Hard violation.

---

## SM-002: Violation Resolution Rate

Definition: the proportion of violations resolved within their expected resolution window.

Formula:

```text
VRR = Resolved Violations (last cycle) / Total Violations Opened (last cycle)
```

Range: 0.0 – 1.0.

Target: ≥ 0.80.

A VRR below 0.50 indicates that violations are accumulating faster than they are being resolved.

---

## SM-003: Exception Renewal Rate

Definition: the proportion of Temporary exceptions that have been renewed at least once.

Formula:

```text
ERR = Renewed Exceptions / Total Temporary Exceptions (last cycle)
```

Range: 0.0 – 1.0.

Target: ≤ 0.20.

A high Exception Renewal Rate indicates that Temporary exceptions are functioning as de facto Structural exceptions.

---

## SM-004: Governance Debt Age

Definition: the average age of open violations and active exceptions, weighted by severity.

Formula:

```text
GDA = Σ(Severity Weight × Age in Days) / Total Open Items
```

Severity weights: Hard = 3, Soft = 2, Advisory = 1.

Target: ≤ 14 days weighted average.

A high Governance Debt Age indicates that governance issues are persisting without resolution.

---

## SM-005: Change Proposal Throughput

Definition: the number of Change Proposals (AED-00012) resolved per governance cycle.

Range: 0 – N.

Target: ≥ 1 per major cycle.

A throughput of 0 over multiple cycles indicates governance stagnation.

---

# 7. Tertiary Metrics

---

## TM-001: AED Coverage Ratio

Definition: the proportion of architectural domains with Published AEDs.

Formula:

```text
ACR = Published AEDs / Total Planned AEDs (as declared in roadmap)
```

Range: 0.0 – 1.0.

Target: 1.0.

A ratio below 1.0 indicates incomplete architectural specification.

---

## TM-002: Contract Coverage Ratio

Definition: the proportion of active Capabilities that expose a Contract.

Formula:

```text
CCR = Capabilities with Contract / Total Active Capabilities
```

Range: 0.0 – 1.0.

Target: 1.0.

A CCR below 0.90 generates a Soft violation.

A CCR below 0.70 generates a Hard violation.

---

## TM-003: Knowledge Freshness Index

Definition: the proportion of Published knowledge artifacts that have been reviewed within their declared review_cycle.

Formula:

```text
KFI = Artifacts Reviewed on Schedule / Total Artifacts with review_cycle
```

Range: 0.0 – 1.0.

Target: ≥ 0.90.

Stale knowledge reduces agent reasoning quality over time.

---

## TM-004: Governance Suite Version Lag

Definition: the number of Core (Class B) AEDs not yet updated to reflect the current Suite Version.

Range: 0 – N.

Target: 0.

A non-zero Suite Version Lag indicates that architectural evolution has outpaced governance documentation.

---

## TM-005: Entropy Index

Definition: a composite measure of architectural entropy, combining:

* duplicated knowledge instances detected (from Layer 3 enforcement);
* undocumented assumptions detected (from Layer 2 enforcement);
* vocabulary drift instances detected (from Layer 3 enforcement);
* runtime artifacts that have persisted beyond their session (from Layer 4 enforcement).

Formula:

```text
Entropy Index = (Duplications × 2 + Undocumented Assumptions × 3 + Vocabulary Drift × 2 + Persistent Runtime × 1) / Total Artifacts
```

Range: 0.0 – N (unbounded).

Target: ≤ 0.05.

An Entropy Index above 0.10 indicates structural degradation requiring immediate architectural review.

The Entropy Index is the most diagnostic metric in the system.

It detects what the Compliance Score does not: slow-moving architectural rot.

---

# 8. Governance Health Index

The Governance Health Index (GHI) is the composite governance health indicator.

It synthesizes Primary and Secondary metrics into a single value.

---

## GHI Formula

```text
GHI = (
    PM-001 × 0.40   +   (Compliance Score)
    PM-002_n × 0.15 +   (Hard Violations normalized: 1 − min(HVC/10, 1))
    PM-003_n × 0.10 +   (Soft Violations normalized: 1 − min(SVC/20, 1))
    PM-004_n × 0.10 +   (Exception Debt: 1 − PM-004)
    SM-001_n × 0.10 +   (Drift Velocity normalized: clamp((DV + 0.1) / 0.2, 0, 1))
    SM-002  × 0.10 +    (Violation Resolution Rate)
    TM-005_n × 0.05     (Entropy: 1 − min(Entropy Index / 0.2, 1))
)
```

Range: 0.0 – 1.0.

---

## GHI Interpretation

| GHI          | Status       | Meaning                                                   |
| ------------ | ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0.90 – 1.00  | EXCELLENT    | Governance is actively improving.                         |
| 0.75 – 0.89  | HEALTHY      | Governance is stable and functioning.                     |
| 0.55 – 0.74  | ACCEPTABLE   | Governance has visible gaps; monitoring required.         |
| 0.35 – 0.54  | DEGRADED     | Governance is weakening; immediate action required.       |
| 0.00 – 0.34  | CRITICAL     | Governance integrity at risk; halt and review.            |

---

## GHI Visibility

The GHI SHALL be:

* computed after every enforcement run;
* exposed in the Repository Registry;
* included in the Boot sequence output;
* recorded in the Metrics History (see Section 9);
* included in every Governance Report (see Section 10).

---

# 9. Metrics History

All metrics SHALL be persisted in a Metrics History store.

---

## History Requirements

The Metrics History SHALL:

* store every enforcement run result;
* preserve metrics at the following granularities: per-run, daily summary, weekly summary, per-Suite-version summary;
* expose trend data for any metric over any historical window;
* never delete historical records.

---

## History Location

```text
governance/
    metrics/
        METRICS-HISTORY.md         ← human-readable summary
        history/
            YYYY-MM-DD-run-NNN.json  ← machine-readable per-run record
        summaries/
            daily/
            weekly/
            suite-version/
```

---

## History Record Format

Each per-run record SHALL contain:

```text
{
  "run_id": "...",
  "timestamp": "...",
  "suite_version": "...",
  "trigger": "...",
  "primary_metrics": { ... },
  "secondary_metrics": { ... },
  "tertiary_metrics": { ... },
  "ghi": ...,
  "compliance_score": ...,
  "hard_violations": ...,
  "soft_violations": ...,
  "active_exceptions": ...,
  "enforcement_report_ref": "..."
}
```

# 10. Governance Report

The enforcement subsystem SHALL produce a Governance Report on scheduled intervals.

---

## Report Cadence

| Report Type       | Trigger                                  |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Per-run summary   | Every enforcement run                    |
| Daily digest      | Daily scheduled enforcement              |
| Architectural     | Every Suite Version increment            |
| Critical alert    | Any GHI transition to DEGRADED/CRITICAL  |

---

## Report Contents

Every Governance Report SHALL include:

* GHI (current and trend)
* Compliance Score (current, previous, delta)
* Hard Violation Count and list
* Soft Violation Count and list
* Active Exception summary
* Exception Debt Score
* Drift Velocity
* Entropy Index
* Key metric trends (7-day, 30-day)
* Recommended actions
* Governance debt summary

---

## Critical Alert

A Critical Alert is triggered when:

* GHI transitions from any state to DEGRADED or CRITICAL;
* Hard Violation Count increases by more than three in a single run;
* Drift Velocity is negative for three consecutive runs;
* Entropy Index exceeds 0.10.

Critical Alerts SHALL be surfaced immediately.

Critical Alerts SHALL not be deferred to the next scheduled report.

---

# 11. Alerting Thresholds

| Metric             | Soft Alert Threshold        | Hard Alert Threshold          |
| ------------------ | --------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| GHI                | < 0.75                      | < 0.55                        |
| Compliance Score   | < 0.90                      | < 0.80                        |
| Hard Violations    | > 0                         | > 3                           |
| Soft Violations    | > 5                         | > 15                          |
| Exception Debt     | > 0.10                      | > 0.15                        |
| Drift Velocity     | Negative (1 run)            | Negative (3 consecutive runs) |
| VRR                | < 0.80                      | < 0.50                        |
| ERR                | > 0.20                      | > 0.40                        |
| Entropy Index      | > 0.05                      | > 0.10                        |
| Contract Coverage  | < 0.90                      | < 0.70                        |
| Knowledge Freshness| < 0.90                      | < 0.70                        |

# 12. Metrics and Governance Evolution

Metrics SHALL inform governance evolution.

The following patterns in metrics data SHOULD trigger Change Proposals (AED-00012):

* Persistent violations in the same layer indicate a missing or underspecified governance rule.
* High Entropy Index with no corresponding violations indicates a detection gap in enforcement.
* High Exception Renewal Rate indicates that a Temporary exception should be Structural, or that the underlying governance rule should be relaxed.
* Sustained negative Drift Velocity with no identifiable root cause indicates a systemic architectural problem.
* Low AED Coverage Ratio indicates that execution is outpacing governance specification.

Metrics are evidence.

Evidence SHALL drive governance decisions.

---

# 13. Metrics Invariants

The following SHALL always remain true.

---

## MET-001

All Primary Metrics SHALL be computed on every enforcement run.

No enforcement run SHALL complete without computing PM-001 through PM-004.

---

## MET-002

The GHI SHALL be computed after every enforcement run.

A stale GHI is a governance risk.

---

## MET-003

Metrics History SHALL be append-only.

Historical records SHALL NOT be modified or deleted.

---

## MET-004

Critical Alerts SHALL be surfaced immediately upon trigger.

Deferred Critical Alerts SHALL be treated as enforcement defects.

---

## MET-005

Metrics SHALL be exposed in the Boot sequence.

An agent initializing the repository SHALL receive the current GHI without additional queries.

---

## MET-006

Metrics SHALL be implementation-independent.

The metric model SHALL not depend on any specific storage, visualization, or reporting technology.

---

## MET-007

Metrics SHALL inform governance decisions.

Governance decisions made without consulting available metrics SHALL be documented as such.

---

## MET-008

The Entropy Index SHALL be computed at minimum on a daily schedule.

An Entropy Index not updated in more than 48 hours SHALL generate a Soft violation.

---

# 14. Architectural Smells

The following patterns indicate metrics degradation.

* enforcement runs completed without computing Primary Metrics;
* GHI not exposed in the Boot sequence;
* Metrics History with gaps or missing runs;
* Critical Alerts deferred or suppressed;
* metrics used to justify inaction rather than inform action;
* Entropy Index consistently ignored at ACCEPTABLE levels while Drift Velocity is negative;
* Governance Reports produced without trend data;
* metrics model tied to a specific tool rather than the canonical model;
* High GHI used to justify skipping enforcement runs;
* metrics treated as reporting artifacts rather than governance instruments.

---

# 15. Future Evolution

The metrics implementation MAY evolve.

Future implementations MAY use:

* time-series databases;
* vector-indexed semantic drift detectors;
* agent-generated trend analysis;
* visual dashboards;
* MCP-integrated metric providers;
* real-time streaming enforcement.

The Governance Health Index formula MAY be recalibrated as the repository matures.

Recalibration SHALL follow the Change Management Process (AED-00012).

Recalibration SHALL preserve comparability with historical GHI values or document the break point explicitly.

---

# 16. Decision

This AED establishes Governance Health Metrics as the observability layer of the repository governance system.

From this point forward:

* the repository always knows its GHI;
* every governance decision has access to trend data;
* entropy is measured, not guessed;
* compliance improvement is verifiable.

The governance system is now closed-loop.

Enforcement produces data.

Data informs decisions.

Decisions update governance.

Updated governance is enforced.

---

# 17. Foundational Principle

> **You cannot improve what you do not measure.**

> **You cannot trust measurement without history.**

> **You cannot act on history without a model.**

This AED provides the model.

The repository now has the means to know not only whether it is governed, but whether it is becoming more governed over time.

That is the difference between governance as a state and governance as a practice.

# AED-00004

# Knowledge Architecture

---

# Status

**Proposed**

Normative.

---

# 1. Purpose

This AED establishes the canonical architecture governing repository knowledge.

Knowledge SHALL be treated as a first-class architectural system.

Documentation is merely one possible representation of knowledge.

Knowledge SHALL NOT be reduced to a collection of Markdown files.

---

# 2. Core Principle

Knowledge exists independently from its representation.

Markdown is a storage medium.

It is not the knowledge itself.

Future implementations MAY replace Markdown with:

* manifests
* databases
* knowledge graphs
* vector indexes
* MCP providers

without changing repository architecture.

---

# 3. Definition

Knowledge is every persistent understanding required for autonomous operation.

Knowledge survives execution.

Knowledge accumulates.

Knowledge evolves.

Knowledge SHALL therefore be governed independently from execution.

---

# 4. Architectural Properties

Repository knowledge SHALL be:

* canonical
* modular
* discoverable
* versionable
* composable
* referenceable
* self-describing

Knowledge SHALL NOT be:

* duplicated
* implicit
* procedural
* runtime-dependent

---

# 5. Knowledge Layers

Knowledge SHALL be organized conceptually into layers.

---

## Layer 0

### Identity

Defines:

* purpose
* scope
* repository identity

Changes rarely.

---

## Layer 1

### Architecture

Defines:

* principles
* AEDs
* repository model
* ontology

Changes infrequently.

---

## Layer 2

### Governance

Defines:

* directives
* policies
* constraints
* standards

Changes occasionally.

---

## Layer 3

### Capability Knowledge

Defines:

* agents
* skills
* workflows
* templates

Changes frequently.

---

## Layer 4

### Project Knowledge

Defines:

* specifications
* architecture
* business rules
* APIs
* decisions

Changes continuously.

---

## Layer 5

### Runtime Context

Defines:

* active work
* current sprint
* temporary state

Changes constantly.

Runtime SHALL NOT become permanent knowledge.

---

# 6. Knowledge Hierarchy

Knowledge SHALL flow downward.

```text
Identity

↓

Architecture

↓

Governance

↓

Capabilities

↓

Project

↓

Runtime
```

Higher layers SHALL remain more stable.

Lower layers SHALL evolve more frequently.

---

# 7. Knowledge Categories

Every knowledge artifact SHALL belong to one category.

---

## Foundational

Defines universal truths.

Examples:

* AEDs
* Ontology
* Vision

---

## Operational

Defines how work happens.

Examples:

* Policies
* Workflows
* Guides

---

## Project

Defines project-specific information.

Examples:

* Requirements
* Specifications
* Roadmaps

---

## Runtime

Defines transient execution state.

Examples:

* Current sprint
* Active tasks
* Working notes

---

## Historical

Preserves evolution.

Examples:

* AED history
* Release history
* Changelogs

---

# 8. Knowledge Lifecycle

Knowledge SHALL possess lifecycle states.

Recommended lifecycle:

```text
Draft

↓

Review

↓

Approved

↓

Published

↓

Deprecated

↓

Archived
```

Knowledge SHALL never exist without status.

---

# 9. Knowledge Dependencies

Knowledge SHALL depend only upon more stable knowledge.

Example:

Project Specification

↓

Architecture

↓

Ontology

Valid.

The reverse dependency SHALL NOT occur.

---

# 10. Knowledge Discovery

Knowledge SHALL never require manual exploration.

Agents SHOULD discover knowledge through repository metadata.

Repository organization SHALL prioritize discoverability over directory aesthetics.

Knowledge SHALL explain where additional knowledge exists.

---

# 11. Knowledge Granularity

Knowledge SHOULD remain atomic.

Each artifact SHOULD answer one architectural question.

Avoid documents attempting to explain everything.

Smaller knowledge units improve:

* maintenance
* discoverability
* reuse
* reasoning

---

# 12. Knowledge Composition

Large concepts SHALL emerge from composition.

Example:

Repository Architecture

↓

Operating Model

↓

Ontology

↓

Governance

↓

Contracts

↓

Capabilities

rather than a single monolithic document.

---

# 13. Canonical References

Knowledge SHALL reference knowledge.

Knowledge SHALL NOT copy knowledge.

Example:

Correct:

> See AED-00003.

Incorrect:

Copy the ontology into another document.

Cross-reference SHALL replace duplication.

---

# 14. Knowledge Invariants

The following SHALL remain true.

---

## INV-001

Knowledge SHALL be canonical.

---

## INV-002

Knowledge SHALL be discoverable.

---

## INV-003

Knowledge SHALL remain independent from execution.

---

## INV-004

Knowledge SHALL possess ownership.

---

## INV-005

Knowledge SHALL expose lifecycle.

---

## INV-006

Knowledge SHALL expose version.

---

## INV-007

Knowledge SHALL expose purpose.

---

## INV-008

Knowledge SHALL expose scope.

---

# 15. Architectural Smells

The following indicate knowledge degradation.

* duplicated documentation
* hidden assumptions
* undocumented concepts
* runtime notes becoming permanent
* project knowledge leaking into governance
* governance duplicated inside capabilities
* architecture explained differently in multiple places

These SHALL be treated as architectural debt.

---

# 16. Repository Memory

The repository SHALL distinguish between:

## Permanent Memory

Stable architectural understanding.

---

## Project Memory

Business understanding.

---

## Runtime Memory

Transient execution state.

---

## Historical Memory

Evolution history.

Each memory class SHALL remain independent.

---

# 17. Future Evolution

Knowledge architecture SHOULD evolve toward graph-like relationships rather than directory hierarchies.

The conceptual model is:

```text
Knowledge

↓

Entities

↓

Relationships

↓

Discovery

↓

Consumption
```

Directories are implementation details.

Relationships are architecture.

---

# 18. Decision

This AED establishes Knowledge as an independent architectural subsystem.

Future repository evolution SHALL optimize:

* discoverability
* composability
* semantic consistency
* progressive loading
* architectural stability

Knowledge SHALL no longer be treated as documentation.

Knowledge SHALL become an executable architectural asset.

---

# 19. Foundational Principle

> Documentation describes software.

> Knowledge enables software.

This repository SHALL prioritize knowledge over documentation.

Documentation becomes one projection of a richer architectural knowledge system.

Knowledge is the asset.

Markdown is merely one transport format.
